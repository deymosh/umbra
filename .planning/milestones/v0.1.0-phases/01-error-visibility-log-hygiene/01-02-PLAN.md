<objective>
Fix the eleven remaining logging call sites in this phase's scope: the six BUG-01 sites that live in self-constructing-logger classes, both BUG-02 scrubbing gaps, BUG-04's silent disconnect handler, and BUG-11's two swallowed session-activation failures.

Purpose: these are the classes where a recording-logger test cannot reach — `LoginViewModel`, `UmbraNostrClient` (and the two files that log through its field), `EventRepositoryImpl`, and `NegentropySyncOrchestrator` all obtain their logger internally via `UmbraLog.tag(TAG)` with no constructor seam. Making them injectable would need per-class Hilt `@Named` qualifiers across production code, which is outside this phase's visibility-only boundary. Output: five error-level handlers in `LoginViewModel`, three in the relay transport trio, one on the wipe path in `EventRepositoryImpl`, and three previously-unscrubbed interpolations routed through `LogScrubber`.

Requirements covered: BUG-01 (6 of its 8 sites; the other 2 landed in Plan 01-01), BUG-02 (all 3 sites), BUG-04, BUG-11.
Decisions applied: D-01 (blanket error level, no manual re-scrub), D-02 (log-only for the wipe path), D-03 (keep `LoginViewModel`'s inner handler, add logging only).
