<objective>
Establish the verification architecture for this phase's logging fixes end-to-end, then apply it to the three use-case classes where a recording logger can actually observe the fix.

Purpose: Requirement BUG-10 (LOG-27) is the only part of this phase whose fix needs a new production dependency — `LogoutUseCase` and `TrimMemoryCachesUseCase` have no logger at all today. That dependency is also what makes decision D-04's recording-logger regression test possible without a Hilt refactor. Proving the whole chain (test double → constructor injection → Hilt `@Provides` wiring → fixed catch → passing assertion) on one small class first means the remaining eleven call-site edits in this phase are mechanical repetitions of a shape already known to compile, wire, and assert.

Output: a `FakeUmbraLogger` recording test double; a constructor-injected `UmbraLogger` on both cleanup use cases wired through `di/UseCaseModule.kt`; twelve per-step cleanup catches and two publish-failure paths converted to error-level logging with the throwable attached; three unit tests that fail if any of those regress back to a silent or throwable-less handler.

Requirements covered: BUG-10 (both classes, all twelve sites), BUG-01 (2 of its 8 sites — the two in `PublishEventUseCases.kt`).
Decisions applied: D-01 (blanket error level, no manual re-scrub), D-02 (log-only, no user-facing surface), D-04 (recording logger test double).
</objective>
