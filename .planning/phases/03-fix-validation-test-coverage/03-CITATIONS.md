**Note on the shared LOG-47/LOG-53/LOG-55 citation:** all three entries point at the single test method above, and this is one test proving three related things together, not three independent proofs. LOG-53's fix (the new-vs-existing decision reading `relayRepository.getAllRelays().first()` rather than the throttled `state.value.relays` mirror) is the one this test's specific scenario (an existing relay seeded into the repository but deliberately absent from `state`) directly isolates — reverting it alone already fails both assertions. LOG-47's fix (re-reading the merge base via `relayRepository.getRelayById(...)` *inside* the per-relay lock, rather than merging against the pre-lock `existingRelay` reference) is exercised by this same test but is not independently isolated by it, since no concurrent mutation happens between the two reads in this single-coroutine scenario — a full revert of the merge branch back to its pre-LOG-42/pre-LOG-47/pre-LOG-53 state does fail this test, but a revert of LOG-47's increment alone (with LOG-53's fix left in place) would not. LOG-55 is the entry recording that this test method didn't exist before this review cycle at all — its citation is the fact of the test's existence, not a specific assertion within it. A future reader should not count this as three independently-verified fixes; it is one regression test covering the cumulative hardening of `saveRelay`'s merge branch across three code-review iterations.
